# Original retro UI tones

The three WAV files are original, deliberately synthesized UI effects derived from the user's supplied sine-envelope code. They are not recordings or CC0 source extracts. Generated by scripts/generate_retro_clicks.py, 48 kHz mono PCM16, 25 ms duration including a short silent tail; original gains are preserved. The approved Fresh click is unchanged under Resources/Sounds/clicks/mouse-click-2.wav. The previous eight studio recordings are retired; the filenames crisp, soft and mechanical are reused for the new sounds to preserve old project IDs.
