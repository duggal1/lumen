Keyboard sound asset attribution
================================

Pack: Cherry MX Blue PBT (bundled under the pack id "mechvibes-default").
Source: https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes/tree/master/soundpacks/keyboard/cherrymx-blue-pbt
Project: Mechvibes - https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes
Author: Mechvibes
License: MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Hai Nguyen

Only the recorded audio asset (sound.ogg -> sound.aiff, PCM16 mono 44.1 kHz) and
its timing definitions are bundled. No Mechvibes source code is used. The audio
was converted from Ogg Vorbis to WAV because Apple's AVAudioFile does not decode
Vorbis; the sample timings in config.json are unchanged from upstream.
