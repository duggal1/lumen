# Director subject lifetime — first implementation

Based on the approved Director plan. Keep the existing 480 Hz solver and all manual controls.

- Deterministic evidence-local subject IDs; distinguish persistent local hover from the bounded remote uncertainty guard. Only an explicit primary click or non-repeat Return/Enter followed by two spatial growth steps and at least five observations may corroborate a sustained result. Competing growing subjects linked to one action remain ambiguous. No AX text inspection or semantic AI classification is claimed.
- Confirmed subjects tolerate a short 1.6s token pause and bypass ordinary shot ceilings. Recorded look-ahead chooses a stable union framing if the full region fits, otherwise stays wide. Never concatenate unrelated visual tracks into an invented pan. Preserve explicit later input and manual/suppressed auto shot policy.
- Only the associated initiating click may be replaced by a corroborated destination shot. Ordinary click behavior remains unchanged, including no-op clicks. No URL recognition or generation-control semantics yet; quiet settling is a heuristic.
- Reuse the derived activity result across camera/style-only replans. Keep native 60Hz on ordinary displays and permit up to 120Hz on supported displays; actual presented cadence not measured.
- Three deterministic synthetic replay tests, including identical pointer stream with growing vs stationary remote activity, pause/long response and independent regions. These are not recordings of the user's demo. Real recorded paired replay, semantic ambiguity (growing remote hover can still mimic output), live/offline shared subject evidence, completion/URL recognition, output-aware webcam/caption geometry and measured display cadence remain outstanding. No claim of flawless intent recognition.

## Handoff correction and acceptance plan

1. Source-time ownership is half-open: a later explicit action terminates, never invalidates, an already corroborated response. Freeze its activity union before the next action; enforce the exclusive end in the planner independent of hold duration. A passive uncorroborated shot still uses the original input guard. Do not resurrect trimmed-out activity.
2. A distinct stationary destination can replace its own click only after a corroborated response, a new primary click, repeated stable non-hover evidence away from the clicked control and old subject, and a unique destination candidate. Ambiguous/no-reveal cases retain ordinary explicit click behavior. Growth-based associations remain conservative, not semantic URL recognition. No change to the live causal solver or manual overrides.
3. Add regressions for an overlapping clicked AX rect, click just outside the prior interval, preceding typing, full synthetic submit/stream/pause/read/click/reveal and no-reveal control, trimmed-out response. Update the two old chain-specific test expectations to the new non-pumping contract; keep explicit input priority assertions. No weakened numeric or performance gates.
4. Remaining actual acceptance: real captured pair, URL/generation completion evidence from supported local observations, ambiguity and delayed-output fixtures, geometry across crops/captions/webcam, actual presented intervals on M1 and high refresh. This synthetic evidence is not hardware or semantic sign-off. Require green full CI on exact head before merge.

The planner now carries protected visual segment IDs into camera scheduling. A following automatic shot cannot anticipate across their end, and the chain builder cannot truncate a protected response to force a full-frame return. Adjacent shots may transition using the original motion equations at the boundary. Regression samples the actual 480 Hz baked track before and after the handoff, asserting stable reading hold and bounded per-tick motion. No claim that presented FPS was measured.

## Real recording proof (opt-in)

Run `bash Tools/director-replay-proof.sh /path/to/project.lumen /path/to/truth.json /path/to/new-output-directory` on a clean macOS checkout. Use an unedited actual capture with autozoom and activity enabled. Annotate the actual submit/click source timestamps, at least two response samples (including the reading beat), destination samples and at least one disjoint hover-only interval. Rectangles are normalized source coordinates [x,y,w,h] and minimumOutputHeight is the independently chosen minimum legible height in a 1080p/portrait output. These numbers must come from the actual recording, not from Director's predicted rectangles. Example structure (illustrative placeholders, not proof):

```json
{"submit":0.5,"click":13.0,"response":[{"time":5.0,"rect":[0.55,0.3,0.15,0.08],"minimumOutputHeight":24},{"time":12.8,"rect":[0.55,0.5,0.15,0.04],"minimumOutputHeight":20}],"destination":[{"time":14.0,"rect":[0.1,0.2,0.2,0.15],"minimumOutputHeight":24}],"hoverOnly":[[16.0,18.0]]}
```

The test copies the source project, runs local analysis, asserts source-time ownership, checks actual derived zoom and projected subject geometry in both 16:9 and 9:16, and saves GPU-rendered PNGs and both 60 fps HEVC movies, validates their encoded frame timestamps, and writes a commit-stamped report with export times. It fails on missing evidence or clipping; it does not assert semantic recognition, caption/webcam occlusion, or presentation intervals. No user recording is available in the remote editing environment. Review PNGs and use actual editor playback on the target Mac with presented-time instrumentation for final cadence sign-off. The normal CI must report this opt-in test as skipped, not real-proof success.
