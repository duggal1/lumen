# Lumen 2026-11-08: Backend + Editing Layer

## Goal
Deliver Sections 1-9 + editing layer without regressing backend.
## Non-goals
No new libraries, no Python, no breadth beyond editing basics.
## Invariants
- Masks in source space/time survive edits.
- Split is output no-op (same frames/audio).
- Zoom region never cropped; min 1.15 max 3.5 continuous.
- Camera/zoom log-space, 60Hz+, no pixel snap.
- Music duck 50ms/400ms, click cues above music.

## Approach
Minimal in-place fixes. No algorithm rewrites where DSP already stable.
